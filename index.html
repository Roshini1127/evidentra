About EVIDENTRA

EVIDENTRA is a research-assistance MVP designed to encourage evidence-led thinking. It helps organize an initial review, find source material and save notes.

Version 1.0 · Educational prototype

EVIDENTRA does not determine factual truth automatically. Its language signals are heuristic indicators and may be wrong. Always inspect original sources, especially for health, safety, legal or financial decisions.
